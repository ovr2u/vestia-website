About us

About Vestia

Vestia is a mediation practice built around one simple principle: a safe space for resolution.

Why Vestia

Our name is inspired by Vesta, the Roman goddess of the hearth and home, a symbol of warmth, stability and continuity. The hearth represents a place where people gather, feel protected, and resolve what matters most. That idea sits at the heart of Vestia: creating a calm, neutral environment where even the most difficult conversations can take place with care and respect.

Our mission is to make mediation more accessible and impactful, by providing flexible solutions tailored to individual needs. We recognise that no two disputes are the same. Whether a matter is personal, commercial, or property-related, we adapt our approach to the people, the context, and the complexity involved.

Disputes can quickly become entrenched and adversarial. Vestia offers something different: a steady, neutral place where people can pause, reflect, and find a way forward.

The team

Our mediators bring over 50 years of combined litigation experience, and the team includes qualified solicitors and barristers. Our mediators are accredited by CEDR and the Society of Mediators, and the team includes CMC Registered Mediators 2026.

Meet our mediators

Our values

Everything we do is guided by six core values. They shape not only how we work, but how parties experience mediation with Vestia: as a thoughtful, respectful, and effective path through conflict.

  • V

    Voice

    Ensuring every party is properly heard, so all perspectives are recognised and respected.

  • E

    Equity

    Maintaining a fair and balanced process, even where power or confidence is unequal.

  • S

    Safety

    Providing a calm, confidential environment where parties can speak openly without fear.

  • T

    Trust

    Building confidence in the process and the mediator, so parties feel secure reaching agreement.

  • I

    Integrity

    Upholding the highest professional and ethical standards, so parties can rely on the process and the mediator with complete confidence.

  • A

    Accountability

    Taking responsibility for delivering a mediation process that encourages collaboration.

How we work

We mix private sessions with each party and joint sessions where they help. If you don't feel able to sit in the same room as the other side, tell us: we can run the whole day in separate rooms, or online.

Our mediators don't decide who is right or give legal advice. Their job is to help each side test its own position, understand the other's, and find terms both can sign.

We mediate across the UK, internationally and online, and we make reasonable adjustments for anyone who needs them. Our accessibility statement explains how.
