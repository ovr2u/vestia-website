How it works

  1. A free call

    Fifteen minutes with a mediator to talk about your dispute and whether mediation fits.

  2. Agreement and preparation

    We fix the date, format and fee. Everyone signs the Mediation Agreement, and the mediator speaks to each side beforehand.

  3. The mediation

    Joint and private sessions, at a pace that suits the people involved. What you tell the mediator privately stays private unless you agree otherwise.

  4. Settlement

    If you reach agreement, it's written up and signed before you leave. Nothing binds anyone until it is in writing and signed.

Fixed fees, quoted per party. A half-day mediation of a dispute worth £20,000 to £100,000 costs £800 + VAT per party, including all preparation and preliminary meetings.

What Vestia stands for

We want mediation to be easier to reach and more useful once you're in the room, with a process shaped around the people and the problem. Six values guide how we work.

  • V

    Voice

    Ensuring every party is properly heard, so all perspectives are recognised and respected.

  • E

    Equity

    Maintaining a fair and balanced process, even where power or confidence is unequal.

  • S

    Safety

    Providing a calm, confidential environment where parties can speak openly without fear.

  • T

    Trust

    Building confidence in the process and the mediator, so parties feel secure reaching agreement.

  • I

    Integrity

    Upholding the highest professional and ethical standards, so parties can rely on the process and the mediator with complete confidence.

  • A

    Accountability

    Taking responsibility for delivering a mediation process that encourages collaboration.
