Start your mediation

Six short questions, about three minutes. Nothing here commits you to anything. It helps us match the right mediator and come back to you with a clear next step, usually within one working day.

What kind of dispute is it? Rough categories are fine. Many disputes are more than one thing.
Where have things got to? Mediation can work at any stage, but this shapes how we set it up.
Roughly what is at stake? A broad band is all we need. It tells us which fee band and format are likely to fit.
How many parties are involved?
How would you prefer to mediate? Choose what appeals. We'll advise on what tends to work best for disputes like yours.
Half day or full day?
How do we reach you? We'll only use these details to reply to this enquiry.

Check and send
