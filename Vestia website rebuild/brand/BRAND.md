# Vestia Mediation — Brand Guidelines (v3, June 2026)

## Logo
- Gemstone diamond mark, split vertically: Warm Grey left, Warm Beige right,
  with three cream leaf/flame forms and a rising-figure dot at the centre.
- Files in this folder: icon (1024 & 2048, transparent), horizontal lockup,
  social avatars (cream background). Never recolour, stretch or add effects.

## Colours
- Charcoal  #3A3A3A  — primary text, wordmark
- Cream     #F9F6F2  — background
- Warm Grey #8A8176  — secondary text, left half of mark
- Warm Beige #D9C7B3 — rules, accents, right half of mark
- Sage      #9AA58E  — quiet accent only (focus states, small highlights)

## Typography
- Quattrocento Sans throughout (Google Fonts), fallback Trebuchet MS / Tahoma.
- Display: regular weight, generous letter-spacing. Wordmark "Vestia" with
  "MEDIATION" in small letterspaced caps beneath/beside.

## Voice
- Strapline: "From Conflict to Clarity."
- Tone: calm, warm, plain-English, professional; client's situation first.
