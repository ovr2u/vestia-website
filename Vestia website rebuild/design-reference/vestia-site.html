Property specialisms

Property disputes are where Vestia's expertise runs deepest. We regularly mediate:

Boundary and rights-of-way disputes; landlord and tenant matters, both residential and commercial; TOLATA and co-ownership disagreements; neighbour disputes, including nuisance and party wall issues; development, construction and defects claims; and disputes between leaseholders, freeholders and managing agents.

Other specialisms

Beyond property, our accredited mediators bring additional specialisms in:

Civil and commercial disputes; workplace and employment matters; family mediation; community disputes; and restorative justice. Whatever the context, the same principles apply: voice, equity, safety, trust, integrity, accountability.

About Vestia

Vestia exists because disputes don't have to end in court. Litigation is slow, expensive and public; mediation is fast, proportionate and private — and it leaves the parties in control of the outcome.

We're committed to making mediation more accessible and more effective, with flexible formats tailored to the people involved. All our mediators are fully accredited and registered with the Civil Mediation Council, with deep experience across property, civil and commercial disputes — and registered with the Civil Mediation Council. The team brings over fifty years of combined litigation experience, including qualified solicitors and barristers.

Fees

Transparent, fixed and quoted per party — with all preparation, administration and preliminary meetings included. A full day is 8 hours; a half day is 4 (the mediation runs continuously, with no deduction for lunch).

Dispute valueLengthFee per partyAdditional hours

The table shows our Specialist Mediator rates. Associate Mediator rates are also available, starting from £300 + VAT per party for a half-day mediation — ask us which is right for your dispute.

Basic fees are payable in full at least seven working days before the mediation day unless otherwise agreed. For in-person mediations, the parties book and pay for the venue (we're happy to suggest options), and fees cover the mediator's travel in most cases — where total travel exceeds five hours, reasonable travel and accommodation costs may apply, always agreed with you in advance. Full terms are set out in our Mediation Agreement, and the price list is available as a PDF download.
