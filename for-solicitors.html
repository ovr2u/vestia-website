How to instruct us

  1. Send us the basics

    The parties, what the dispute is about, its rough value, and any dates that matter: a CCMC, the end of a stay, a limitation deadline. Email enquiries@vestiamediation.co.uk, call 0330 133 5199, or use the intake form or booking request form.

  2. Conflict check and proposal

    We check for conflicts and come back with a proposed mediator, available dates and a fixed fee. The mediator discloses any prior dealings with either party before anyone signs.

  3. Mediation Agreement

    All parties sign our Mediation Agreement. The basic fee is paid at least seven working days before the mediation. Under the agreement, each party's solicitors are liable for any unpaid fees due from their client.

  4. Preparation

    The mediator will ask for a short position statement and the key documents, and will usually speak to each side beforehand. Those calls are included in the fee.

  5. The day, and afterwards

    Any settlement is written up and signed before everyone leaves, so it helps to bring a draft agreement or consent order you can adapt. If the parties want, the mediator can keep working with them after the day.

Frequently asked questions

Formats

Online, in person or hybrid

Online mediations run on video, with the mediator as host and separate breakout rooms for each side. Online needs no venue, so it is usually the quickest to arrange. For in-person days the parties book and pay for the venue, and we're happy to suggest options. Telephone works for short follow-up sessions.

Half day, full day or longer

A half day is four hours and a full day eight, run continuously with no deduction for lunch. Larger disputes can run over several days. For claims under £20,000 we offer an abridged online mediation at a lower cost.

Style

Most of our mediations mix private sessions with joint sessions where they help, and some run entirely in separate rooms. Our mediators can work purely facilitatively, or take a more evaluative approach and reality-test the legal positions where both sides want that.

Fee certainty

Fees are fixed per party by the value of the dispute and include all preparation, administration and preliminary meetings. You'll know the full cost before the agreement is signed. Time beyond the booked half or full day is charged at the hourly rate shown. Travel is included unless the mediator would have to travel for more than five hours in total, and any such costs are agreed in advance.

Availability

Tell us the window you're working to and we'll come back with dates for the mediators who fit, usually within one working day. If you have a hearing or a stay deadline, say so in your first email and we'll work back from it.

We mediate across the UK, internationally and online. Gurprit Mattu accepts appointments in England and internationally, in all formats, and is regularly instructed in matters involving offshore structures and parties in more than one jurisdiction.

Co-mediation

Two mediators can work together where that helps the parties. It tends to earn its cost in multi-party disputes, in cases where technical property issues sit alongside a family or business relationship, and in high-conflict matters where a second mediator can keep every room moving.

Typical pairings are a property specialist with a commercial barrister, or a lead mediator with an associate mediator. Gurprit Mattu and Amy Kaur both have experience as co-mediators. Co-mediation is priced on request.
