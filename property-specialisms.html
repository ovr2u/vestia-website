Property specialisms

Property dispute mediation

Property disputes can be complex and highly technical. Mediators who already understand the legal side grasp the issues faster, which saves valuable time on the mediation day.

Property knowledge and experience

Our specialist property mediators have together amassed more than three decades of property law experience, and are therefore fully equipped to tackle a wide variety of property disputes.

Below is a list of issues we commonly see resolved through mediation, but please reach out if you have a dispute that is not listed, since it is very likely we can still help.

Your property mediators

Lucie-Anne Rhodes spent over fourteen years in property litigation, latterly as Head of Legal for the UK's largest property management and real estate services provider.

Claudia Haisman-Green was a Commercial Real Estate solicitor for more than two decades, much of it in senior roles at leading international firms.

Commercial landlord and tenant

  • rent arrears and other breaches of lease
  • forfeiture
  • debt recovery
  • dilapidations
  • break options
  • rent reviews
  • lease renewals
  • rent deposits
  • service charges
  • consent to dealings (i.e. change of use, alterations, assignment and sub-letting)

Residential landlord and tenant

  • breach of lease
  • possession claims
  • housing disrepair
  • rights of first refusal
  • enfranchisement
  • consents to dealings (i.e. alterations, assignment and sub-letting)

Residential property management

  • service charge disputes
  • estate management
  • consultation/dispensation issues
  • 18-month rule
  • right to manage
  • named/embedded manager issues

Other property-related disputes

  • building safety (including Remediation Orders and Remediation Contribution Orders)
  • rights of way / easements
  • telecommunications
  • trespassers
  • nuisance
  • rights to light
  • mortgage-related issues
  • planning issues
  • adverse possession, boundary disputes, party wall issues and other neighbour disputes

Facilitating property transactions and projects

We can help to facilitate complex property transactions and projects (e.g. construction, stalled developments), breaking deadlocks and keeping things moving even when direct communication has become difficult.
