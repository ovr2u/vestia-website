Service description

A personalised mediation consultation, where you can discuss your specific needs with a mediator, ask any questions (e.g. about the mediation process) and determine whether Vestia Mediation is right for you.

Format: telephone call or online meeting.

Cancellation policy

To cancel or reschedule a call, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance.

Good to have to hand
