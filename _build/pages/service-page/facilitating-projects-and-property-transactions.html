Service description

Not every property transaction runs smoothly. Disagreements after a survey, disputes between co-owners, breakdowns between buyer and seller, or chains under pressure can all bring things to a standstill. We work as a neutral third party to help everyone involved find a workable way forward, keeping the deal alive and avoiding the cost and delay of a formal dispute.

We recognise that no two transactions are the same, so this service can be very flexible to suit your needs.
