How it works

  1. A free call

    Fifteen minutes with a mediator to talk about your dispute and whether mediation fits.

  2. Agreement and preparation

    We fix the date, format and fee. Everyone signs the Mediation Agreement, and the mediator speaks to each side beforehand.

  3. The mediation

    Joint and private sessions, at a pace that suits the people involved. What you tell the mediator privately stays private unless you agree otherwise.

  4. Settlement

    If you reach agreement, we encourage you to put it in writing and sign it before you leave. The agreement is yours, and nothing binds anyone until it is in writing and signed.

Fixed fees, quoted per party. A half-day mediation of a dispute worth £20,000 to £100,000 costs £800 + VAT per party, including all preparation and preliminary meetings.
