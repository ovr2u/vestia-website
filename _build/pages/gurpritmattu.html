Profile

Gurprit is a fully-accredited mediator and a dual-qualified barrister practising in England & Wales (2007) and the British Virgin Islands (2019), with a specialist practice in high-value commercial and private disputes. She has extensive experience in shareholder and partnership conflicts, debt disputes, fraud and misrepresentation cases, and mis-selling claims. Her cross-border practice means she is regularly instructed in matters involving international parties, offshore structures, and multi-jurisdictional issues, expertise that directly informs her work as a mediator.

Gurprit is skilled at reality-testing legal positions, clarifying risk, and helping parties evaluate the true cost of continuing litigation. She brings structured process discipline to negotiations where emotions, sunk costs, or strategic posturing may otherwise derail progress. As a practising barrister, she has appeared across a wide range of commercial and civil matters, giving her a clear understanding of litigation risk, evidential pressure points, and the commercial realities that shape settlement. She also mediates private family disputes, employment and probate matters, where her balanced, empathetic style helps parties move past entrenched positions and towards workable, future-focused outcomes.

Additionally, Gurprit is trained in restorative justice mediation, enabling her to assist in disputes where there are parallel allegations of criminal conduct, provided there are no safeguarding concerns. This includes sensitive employment matters involving allegations such as sexual harassment, and family disputes where there has been violence. Her restorative practice offers parties a structured, safe, and constructive space to address harm, accountability, and resolution. These can be in a group setting.

With Indian heritage and fluency in Punjabi and Hindi, Gurprit is able to conduct mediations in multiple languages and is particularly well-placed to assist parties from South Asian communities. Her international background and BVI practice further equip her to mediate disputes involving offshore companies, cross-border investments, and culturally diverse parties.

Gurprit accepts appointments as a mediator in England and internationally, in all formats. She is valued for combining legal precision with a practical, human-centred approach, helping parties reach resolution efficiently and with dignity. She has experience as both a solo and co-mediator.
