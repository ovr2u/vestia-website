Other areas of specialist knowledge and experience

Particular specialisms and areas of focus include commercial and civil claims, private family disputes, employment matters, and restorative justice.

Below is a list of issues we commonly see resolved through mediation, but please reach out if you have a dispute that is not listed, since it is very likely we can still help.

Who handles these disputes

Gurprit Mattu is a dual-qualified barrister who mediates commercial, cross-border, probate, employment and private family disputes, and is trained in restorative justice. She can mediate in Punjabi and Hindi.

Amy Kaur focuses on employment, community and private family mediation. Lucie-Anne Rhodes and Claudia Haisman-Green also mediate wider civil, commercial, partnership and corporate claims.

Commercial claims

  • Contract disputes (breach, interpretation, performance)
  • Partnership, shareholder and joint venture disputes
  • Sale of goods and supply chain disputes
  • Professional negligence claims
  • Debt recovery and unpaid invoices
  • Fraud and misrepresentation cases
  • Mis-selling claims

Civil claims

  • Debt claims
  • Consumer complaints and county court small claims
  • Personal injury claims
  • Contentious probate and Inheritance Act 1975 claims
  • Trust disputes
  • Defamation and reputation claims
  • Disputes between members of clubs, associations or charities

Private family disputes

  • Drafting parenting plans
  • Inheritance and contentious probate disputes between relatives
  • Disputes over wills and lifetime gifts
  • Family business succession and ownership fallouts between adult relatives
  • Disputes between adult siblings over care of, or finances for, elderly parents
  • Disputes over funeral arrangements and the estate's administration
  • Adult family conflicts not involving children's arrangements or divorce finances

Employment matters

  • Unfair, wrongful and constructive dismissal claims
  • Breach of employment contract claims (post-termination)
  • Unlawful deduction from wages and unpaid notice/holiday pay
  • Redundancy payment and selection disputes
  • Settlement agreement negotiations and negotiated exits
  • Post-termination restrictive covenant disputes
  • Bonus, commission and share scheme disputes
  • Director and senior executive exit disputes

Restorative justice

We can also assist in disputes where there are parallel allegations of criminal conduct, provided there are no safeguarding concerns.

This includes sensitive employment matters involving allegations such as sexual harassment, and family disputes where there has been violence.

We offer parties a structured, safe, and constructive space to address harm, accountability, and resolution. These can be in a group setting.

Community and family mediation

Amy Kaur has been appointed as a mediator of the Sikh Court, where she has undertaken community family disputes. Amy is training to become an accredited family mediator, so she cannot accept family cases that have been ordered by the court or are funded by Legal Aid or the Voucher Scheme.
