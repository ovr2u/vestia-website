Confidential to the mediator. Without prejudice.

This form goes to your mediator only. We will not share what you write with the other side, or with anyone else, without your permission, except where our Mediation Agreement allows it (for example, where the law requires disclosure). Everything you provide is part of the mediation, so it is confidential and without prejudice: it can't be referred to or relied on in court if the dispute doesn't settle, unless it would have been admissible anyway.

Take your time. Short answers are fine and you can leave out anything you'd rather discuss with the mediator directly. Pressing send opens an email in your own email app with your answers set out in full. Email isn't encrypted end to end, so if you'd prefer another way to send this, or need to send documents securely, ask us and we'll arrange it.

About you

The mediation

Parties and representatives

Under the Mediation Agreement, everyone attending must be named in advance, and at least one person on each side must have full authority to settle.

Names of everyone on each side of the dispute, and which of them you are or represent.
Solicitors and counsel for each party, if you know them, with firm names.
Names and roles, including anyone coming to support you.

Brief background

A few paragraphs is plenty. Your mediator will also read any position statement or documents.
Any offers by either side and roughly when. This stays with the mediator.

Outcome sought

Include anything that isn't about money, such as an apology, a timetable for works, or keeping a working relationship.
For example certainty, cost, timing, reputation or a relationship you need to keep.

Anything the mediator should know

For example tensions between the people involved, topics to avoid in a joint session, health or wellbeing concerns, or any worries about safety.
Are you comfortable meeting the other side in a joint session? Many mediations happen mostly or entirely in separate rooms. There's no wrong answer.
Interpreters (including BSL), extra breaks, venue access, large print or anything else that would help.
