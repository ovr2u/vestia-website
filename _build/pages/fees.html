About this service

Fees

Read-in fee (documents up to 250 pages): £450 + VAT per party

Basic fee, covering meetings and calls of up to 4 hours in total:
Transactions under £2 million: £525 + VAT per party
£2 million to £5 million: £900 + VAT per party
£5 million to £10 million: £1,200 + VAT per party
Over £10 million: price on request

Additional hours (transactions under £2 million): £105 + VAT per hour per party

We'll agree a quote with you before any work starts, and we're open to discussing fees for unusual or complex transactions.

Payment and practical terms

Payment

Basic fees must be paid in full at least seven working days in advance of the mediation day unless otherwise agreed. Any additional hours are invoiced after the mediation.

In-person mediations

Unless otherwise agreed, the parties will be responsible for booking the venue and paying any associated costs. If you have questions about venue options, please feel free to get in touch as we may be able to assist.

In most cases, the above costs will cover all travel expenses. If the mediator is required to travel for more than 5 hours in total, you may be asked to cover reasonable travel and accommodation costs. Any such costs will be agreed with you in advance.

Cancellation

Once a date is agreed, cancelling or postponing costs 25% of the basic fee if more than seven working days' notice is given, 50% with three to seven working days' notice, and the full basic fee with fewer than three working days' notice, plus VAT.

All other terms and conditions are set out in our Mediation Agreement.
